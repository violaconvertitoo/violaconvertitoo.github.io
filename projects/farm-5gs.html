Farm 5Gs Checklist – Multi-Country Survey Instrument

A digital farm-level assessment tool developed for multi-country implementation, with validation and automated scoring.

KoboXLSFormSurvey QACross-sectional data

A structured digital farm-assessment tool supporting whole-farm improvement and efforts to reduce the need for antimicrobials.

Why the tool matters

The Farm 5Gs approach supports structured assessment of livestock farm practices, connecting advisory work and practical improvement areas relevant to animal health and antimicrobial use.

From framework to a field-ready instrument

I worked on the digital implementation of a structured livestock-farm assessment checklist in KoboToolbox, translating domain-specific questions into a usable survey with validation, skip patterns and automated calculations. This included handling non-applicable responses and ensuring that denominators reflect applicable questions.

Scoring transparency

A domain score depends on affirmative responses and the number of applicable questions. Not-applicable answers reduce the possible maximum rather than being treated as negative responses; this distinction is essential for interpreting the overall compliance percentage.

What I developed

  • Implemented and revised the digital questionnaire in Kobo/XLSForm, including skip logic, field constraints and multilingual survey elements.
  • Developed and checked weighted domain scoring with explicit treatment of not-applicable responses.
  • Configured total points, adjusted maximum score and compliance percentages.
  • Supported testing, data validation and subsequent analytical workflows.

Scoring logic

For each domain, the score is calculated from the share of applicable responses marked “Yes”, multiplied by that domain’s weight. Not-applicable answers are excluded from the denominator; domains with no applicable answers contribute no available maximum. Overall compliance is total earned points divided by the adjusted maximum, where positive.
