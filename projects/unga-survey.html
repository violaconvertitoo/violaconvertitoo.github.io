UNGA 2024 AMR Commitments – Stakeholder Survey

Designed a digital survey to capture stakeholder perspectives on progress, barriers and priorities for implementing global AMR commitments.

KoboSurvey designQualitative evidenceOne Health

A digital survey capturing stakeholder perspectives on progress, barriers and priorities for implementation of the 2024 UNGA antimicrobial-resistance commitments.

Purpose

The questionnaire collects informed stakeholder perspectives on implementation progress toward 2029, including enablers, barriers, contributions and areas requiring acceleration. It complements rather than replaces official country monitoring.

Instrument design and respondent experience

I translated the stakeholder assessment of progress on the 2024 UN General Assembly antimicrobial-resistance commitments into a Kobo survey. The instrument captures respondents’ perspectives on implementation, barriers, contributions and priority areas across One Health sectors; it is not an official country-level evaluation.

Question designDomain-specific maturity ratings and multiple-choice prioritization
LogicConditional visibility, choice filters and exclusive-response constraints
Data qualityUnable-to-assess options, relevance checks and response validation
PurposeComparable stakeholder feedback to support evidence synthesis

The public form can be explored without access to respondents' confidential submissions.

A survey designed for honest uncertainty

Respondents are asked to assess only areas they know well. Explicit “Unable to assess” and “Not applicable” responses reduce pressure to guess, while filtering keeps questions relevant to selected domains.

What I implemented

  • Translated a structured questionnaire into a deployable Kobo survey.
  • Built domain-specific conditional questions and selective display logic.
  • Applied response constraints, including mutually exclusive options and limits on the number of priorities selected.
  • Added “Unable to assess” and “Not applicable” options to discourage unsupported estimates.
  • Tested survey flow, labels and submission experience.

Survey design principles

Respondents are asked to assess only areas they know sufficiently well, provide examples where possible and distinguish implementation maturity from insufficient information. The survey is designed for stakeholder evidence rather than official performance ranking.
