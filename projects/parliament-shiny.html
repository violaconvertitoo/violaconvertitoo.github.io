Where Are the Rural Voices? – Interactive R Shiny Map

Interactive mapping of rural-born parliamentarians across six European countries, 1999–2019.

R ShinysfleafletInteractive map

An interactive exploration of rural birthplace and parliamentary representation across Germany, France, Spain, Ireland, Czechia and Austria.

Research question

How has the share of national parliamentarians born in rural areas changed over time, and how does it vary across selected European countries?

Technical implementation and analytical boundaries

This R Shiny project brings together a spatial view of six European countries and a linked distribution of parliamentarians by birthplace category. Users can change the legislative year, select a country and compare rural-born shares over time (1999–2019).

TechnologyR, Shiny, sf, leaflet and interactive data visualization
CoverageGermany, France, Spain, Ireland, Czech Republic and Austria
InteractionsTime slider, country selection, choropleth and party-level histogram
LimitationBirthplace is not current residence or a population-adjusted measure of representation

The descriptive pattern motivates further research into political recruitment and rural origins, but cannot establish discrimination, unequal opportunities or causal effects.

How to explore the results

Start by choosing a legislative year. Hover over or select a country to inspect its rural-born share, then compare birthplace categories across political parties in the linked chart. Use the slider to observe descriptive changes across the available years.

Data & scope

The project explores parliamentarians’ birthplace classifications across six European countries and legislative years from 1999 to 2019. It is a descriptive spatial analysis, not an estimate of electoral discrimination or causal political exclusion.

How to use the map

  • Choose a year: use the time slider to compare legislative periods.
  • Select a country: inspect the composition of parliamentarians by birthplace category and party.
  • Read the map: country shading represents the share of parliamentarians born in rural areas.
  • Compare patterns: explore differences across countries and over time.

Selected descriptive observations

In the project analysis, Ireland stands out with a relatively high rural-born share compared with the other countries. The original write-up reports a decline from approximately 50% in 1999 to 37.69% in 2019. These are descriptive project findings and should be interpreted alongside data coverage and classification choices.

Interpretation & limitations

Birthplace is not current residence, constituency, or necessarily rural political representation. Establishing underrepresentation would require a suitable population or candidate benchmark. The project raises questions for further research but does not establish barriers to political office.

My technical work

Developed an interactive mapping workflow in R using Shiny and spatial visualization tools, combining a choropleth map, country-level interactions, party-level plots and a year slider.
